Imposed Entertainment knowledge base

This is the public information source for Imposed Entertainment and its support assistant. The pages linked here are intended for visitors and public search crawlers. Private artist, label, account, earnings and release-management data is never public knowledge.

What is Imposed Entertainment?

Imposed Entertainment is an independent music label portal with separate experiences for listeners, artists and label members. Public visitors can discover published artists, open public artist and release pages, visit the shop, and read help and policy information. Invited artists and label members use a separate authenticated workspace to manage permitted work.

Discover music and public artist pages

Start at Discover music to browse published artist pages and releases. Public artist pages are available at /listen/artist/{artist-slug} when the artist has published their profile. A public page may link to external streaming services; availability and playback depend on the destination service.

  • Only published public artist content should appear in discovery.
  • If a page fails to load, refresh and complete any Cloudflare security check shown.
  • Do not share private account or workspace URLs as public artist pages.

Listener accounts and saved music

Listening and discovery do not require an artist or label account. A listener account is optional and is separate from the invited artist/label workspace. Use Listener account for supported saved-library and account features. Device-only saved choices may not sync between browsers until signed in.

Artist and label workspace

The artist/label portal is invitation-only and is accessed at Artist and label sign in. Its private data can include drafts, submissions, roster details, notifications, account preferences, statements and other workspace records. These details require authentication and authorization and must not be copied into a public chatbot knowledge source.

Shop and policies

Visit the shop for public catalogue information. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for information about the site's policies. Product availability, pricing and fulfilment may change and should be checked on the relevant live page.

Support and troubleshooting

The Help Centre covers discovery, saved music, listener accounts and common issues. Never send passwords, session tokens, payment-card details, private release links or other secrets to an AI assistant. The support assistant can explain public features but should not claim to inspect private records unless a verified, permission-scoped integration explicitly supports that action.

AI knowledge-source guidance

For public knowledge ingestion, use this page and the linked public pages as website sources. The source should be able to fetch public HTML without logging in. Do not configure the crawler with an artist's session, cookies, service-role key or other credentials. Keep authenticated pages, admin routes, private documents and user records out of the knowledge source. Refresh the source after public content changes.
