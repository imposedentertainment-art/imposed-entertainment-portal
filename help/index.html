OFFICIAL HELP CENTRE

Good music. Fewer headaches.

Find straightforward answers about discovering artists, public profiles, saved music, listener accounts and the Imposed Entertainment artist workspace.

Discover and listen

Visit Discover artists to browse the public roster and open artist pages. A short Cloudflare security check may be required before public catalogue content loads. It is not an artist or listener account sign-in.

  • Only artists who have published their public profile appear in the directory.
  • Streaming is provided through the listening links shown on each artist or release page.
  • If an artist page does not load, refresh and complete the security check again.

Save music and follow artists

You can save artists and releases while browsing. Before signing in, these choices may be stored only in the current browser. Create or sign in to a listener account to sync supported library items between devices.

  • Use the library controls to view or remove saved items.
  • Follow an artist to keep up with new releases where notifications are available.
  • Export or import your collection only using a file you trust.

Listener accounts

Listener accounts are optional and separate from the invite-only artist and label workspace. A listener account is for your music library and listener features; it does not grant access to release submission, finances, private files or label tools.

Open Listener account to sign in, create an account or manage your listener session.

Artist and label workspace

The artist/label workspace is separate and invite-only. Use Artist / label sign in and the email address that received your invitation. If you have not been invited, the listener account cannot be upgraded into a workspace role by changing a profile field.

Signed-in artists and label members can use the in-portal Support page to contact the team. Never include passwords, one-time codes, API keys or recovery codes in a support message.

Public artist profiles

Artist pages display only the information an artist has chosen to publish. If a profile or a detail is missing, it may be private or hidden by the artist's visibility preferences. The site should not expose private release drafts, internal notes, earnings or workspace files on a public page.

Artists can review their public profile and visibility options after signing in to their workspace.

Shop and orders

Visit the shop to browse available catalogue items. Product availability, fulfilment and delivery depend on the specific item and provider. Do not assume an order has been placed unless the shop confirms it.

Artist shop links may lead to third-party stores, which handle their own checkout and delivery.

Common questions

Do I need an account to browse artists?

No artist or listener account is required to start browsing. The site may ask you to complete a security check to protect public artist pages from automated abuse. An account is useful for syncing supported saved items.

Why can’t I open a profile or see a release?

Check the spelling of the URL and retry the security check. The artist may have unpublished the profile or hidden the release. Only published public information should appear in listener discovery.

Can a listener account access artist tools?

No. Listener accounts are intentionally separated from artist and label workspace roles. Workspace access is invite-only and checked by the server; changing client-side settings cannot grant it.

What should I include when reporting a bug?

Describe the page, what you expected, what happened, the approximate time, your browser/device and the exact error message if one appeared. Remove personal information. Never send passwords, security codes, session tokens or private release files.

Does the AI assistant have access to my private account data?

The support assistant should only receive the information and tools explicitly authorised for it. It must not request passwords or security codes. Account-specific diagnostics must be checked by a trusted server for the signed-in user, not granted by a chat message or browser-side flag.

Still stuck? Signed-in artists and label members can open Support in the workspace to send the team a message. For public listener issues, include only the minimum non-sensitive details needed to explain the problem.
