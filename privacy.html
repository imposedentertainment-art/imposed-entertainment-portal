Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Imposed Entertainment (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Imposed Entertainment website, artist portal and public artist pages (the “Service”). Please read it before using the Service.

Who operates the Service?
Imposed Entertainment operates this Service. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Imposed Entertainment team through the official contact channel provided to you or with your artist/label invitation. We do not publish a separate privacy email address here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and invitation details: email address, display name, account role, invitation status and information you or the label team enter into the portal.
  • Sign-in information: when you sign in through a third-party provider such as Google, X or Discord, we receive the account identifiers and profile information that the provider makes available and that you authorize. Your email address may not be supplied by every provider or account. We do not receive your provider password.
  • Artist, music and podcast information: profile photo, biography, links, location if supplied, music release metadata, podcast show and episode titles/descriptions, audio files, artwork, planned publish dates, rights confirmations, review notes, Spotify links, submissions, status updates, comments and other information you upload or the label team records. Podcast audio and artwork submitted for review are stored in private file storage and are not automatically published by this portal.
  • Technical and security information: basic request and device/browser information, authentication/session data, security-verification results and logs needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact the team.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify invitations; provide artist and label workspace features; manage music and podcast submissions and communications; display public artist pages and release information you choose to publish; respond to requests; prevent abuse; maintain service reliability; and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Public artist pages

Some profile information and released-music details can be published on a public artist page if the relevant publishing options are enabled. Public information may be viewed, copied or indexed by other people and search engines. Do not publish personal information you do not want to make public. Private drafts, earnings, tasks and label notes are not intended to appear on public artist pages.

4. Third-party services, distribution and AI support

The Service uses service providers to host, secure and deliver its features. Depending on the feature you use, these may include Supabase for authentication, databases and file storage; Cloudflare for hosting and security; and Google, X or Discord if you choose those sign-in methods. Those providers process relevant information under their own terms and privacy policies.

RouteNote and music distribution: If you submit a release for distribution or use a RouteNote-related workflow, relevant release and delivery information may be shared with RouteNote and, where required for distribution, its digital service provider and platform partners. This may include artist and release names, track and album metadata, audio files, artwork, contributor and rights information, identifiers, release dates and contact or account details needed to process a release. RouteNote and distribution partners may use this information to review, deliver, administer, report on or support the release, under their own terms and privacy notices. Submitting information to our portal does not itself guarantee acceptance, distribution, a release date, placement or revenue. You should only submit material and personal information you are authorized to provide.

Botpress and AI support: If you use an AI-powered support assistant, the text you enter and relevant conversation context may be sent to Botpress and its configured infrastructure or AI/model providers so that the assistant can generate a response, maintain the conversation and help resolve support questions. Please do not enter passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, confidential unreleased material, sensitive personal information or other information you do not want processed by these providers. AI responses can be inaccurate and should be checked; do not treat them as legal, financial, rights-clearance or distribution guarantees. Support conversations may be logged or retained for service operation, troubleshooting, security and quality review according to the configuration and applicable provider retention practices. We will not intentionally send private label records or uploaded files to the assistant unless the feature is configured to provide that context for the support request.

RouteNote, Botpress, model providers and other third parties act under their own terms and privacy policies for their services. Their processing may take place in countries other than yours, subject to applicable safeguards where required. We do not control their independent practices or retention periods. Check their current privacy documentation before using related features.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized Imposed Entertainment personnel who need it to run the label portal; service providers supporting the Service, including the providers described above; RouteNote and relevant distribution partners when needed for a release or distribution workflow; Botpress and configured AI/model providers when you use AI support; people you choose to share it with or the public when you publish it; and authorities or other parties where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce applicable terms or comply with law. We aim to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Cookies, storage and sessions

The Service uses browser storage and authentication/session mechanisms to keep you signed in, remember necessary settings and support security. A service worker may cache public website assets for performance and limited offline behaviour. Avoid using shared devices for private account access, and sign out when finished.

7. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service, maintain label and release records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or comply with legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by information type. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can review and update certain profile information in the portal, change public profile settings, unlink supported sign-in identities where the authentication provider permits it, or ask us to correct or delete information. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact the Imposed Entertainment team through the official contact channel provided to you. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required or permitted by law.

9. Children

The Service is intended for invited artists and label members and is not designed for children who are below the minimum age required to use the Service or its authentication providers. Do not use the Service if you are not eligible to do so.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. The “Last updated” date above identifies the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised Policy.

11. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact Imposed Entertainment through the official contact channel supplied with your invitation or by your label contact.
